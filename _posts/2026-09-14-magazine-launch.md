🎉 We are excited to share TAPPA's first community magazine — about the people, the stories and the everyday life around our research here in Tübingen.

In this issue: a conversation with **Philipp Hennig** on probability, patience and machines that know what they don't know; a community survey on **flying to conferences** and how we feel about it; talks from the **FridayTalks** archive; and some very serious **ice cream reviews** 🍦
