The Tübingen AI Center, the ELLIS Institute Tübingen, the Cluster of Excellence “Machine Learning: New Perspectives for Science”, ELIZA, and TAPPA (Tübingen AI PhD and Postdoc Assembly) are pleased to invite you to the Tübingen AI Winter Poster Event 2026.

The event will showcase recent ML papers from Tübingen published in top-tier venues.

If your paper has been accepted since our previous event (summer 2026), we warmly invite you to present your poster! Otherwise, feel free to join us to mingle, exchange ideas, and enjoy the afternoon with snacks and drinks.

*When*: Wednesday, December 2 @ 14:00-18:00
*Where*: MvL1 (Maria-von-Linden-Straße 1, Tübingen) – Top Floor
